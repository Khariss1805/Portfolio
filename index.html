What I work on

The part of the business where customer experience meets data.

  • CX metrics that mean something

    Finding which factors move CSAT and FCR, so teams fix causes instead of chasing averages.

  • Quality assurance

    Scorecards, evaluator calibration and QA results that agents and team leads trust.

  • Dashboards and reporting

    Power BI and Tableau reports with clear definitions, built for the person who reads them every Monday.

  • SQL and data preparation

    Joining tickets, surveys and QA records in MySQL or PostgreSQL, and checking the data before trusting it.

  • Forecasting and staffing

    Volume forecasts and Erlang C staffing in Excel and Python, explained without jargon.

  • Research and design

    UX research and visual design from my Multimedia Engineering degree, used to make reports easy to read.

About me

I started in customer service: selling in Córdoba, Argentina, then taking calls and chats for international clients at Sitel and TaskUs. Every shift ended with a CSAT score, an AHT number and a QA result, and I wanted to know what was behind them.

That curiosity took me from a degree in Multimedia Engineering to a specialisation in Business Intelligence with an emphasis on Big Data, which I am finishing now at Universidad Autónoma de Occidente.

I work in Spanish, English (C2) and Portuguese (B2). I like the moment when a messy export becomes one clear chart, and the meeting where it changes what the team does next.
