About me

I studied Multimedia Engineering, where I learned to design for the person on the other side of the screen. Then I wanted to measure whether a design actually worked, which led me to a specialisation in Business Intelligence with an emphasis on Big Data.

My earlier jobs were with people: sales, customer support for international companies, community management and content. They taught me how people really use digital products, and where they get stuck.

I work in Spanish, English (C2) and Portuguese (B2). I like the moment a messy export becomes one clear chart, and the moment that chart changes what a team builds next.
