NonaAntojos

A mobile web menu for a local catering business, designed so busy customers can find and order what they want in a few taps.

Role
UX designer and content creator
Status
Design concept
Method
User research and rapid prototyping
Tools
Figma
See the prototypes in Figma (opens in a new tab)

The challenge

Most of NonaAntojos’ customers order on their phone, often in a hurry. The old menu was a long list that was hard to read on a small screen.

The goal: a menu that is clear and pleasant to browse, with categories and popular dishes easy to reach, so ordering takes as little time as possible.

How we worked

  1. Research: a persona of a busy professional who orders for herself and her team.
  2. Information architecture: the menu content broken down, then organised into a clear structure.
  3. Flows: a task flow and user flows for the most common goal, placing an order.
  4. Prototypes: mid-fidelity screens in Figma to test layout and navigation.

My role

I led the UX work and wrote the menu content: research, structure, flows and prototypes. I kept the layout simple, the text short and the tap targets large, so the menu works well on any phone.
