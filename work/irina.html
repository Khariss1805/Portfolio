La resiliencia de Irina

A VR narrative game that puts players in the shoes of Irina, a young woman facing gender-based violence, to build empathy through an experience rather than a lecture.

Role
UX designer and content creator
Team
Multimedia Engineering student team
Method
Design thinking in iterative sprints
Tools

The challenge

Gender-based violence is hard to talk about, and awareness campaigns are easy to scroll past. We wanted people to feel the emotional weight of the problem, and remember it.

Virtual reality makes that possible, but it raises the bar: the story has to be strong, interaction has to be intuitive for people new to VR, and the experience must move players without harming them emotionally.

How we worked

  1. Empathise: research into the problem and the people involved, summarised in personas for the client and the player.
  2. Define: a stakeholder map and a SWOT analysis to understand who the experience serves and what could limit it.
  3. Ideate: a minimum viable product board that prioritised the scenes and mechanics that mattered most.
  4. Prototype: a journey map of Irina’s emotional arc, then high-fidelity prototypes of every screen and decision.
  5. Test: playtests in each sprint to adjust pacing, choices and emotional intensity.

My role

I designed the user experience and wrote content: the journey map, the decision flows, on-screen copy and the visual style of the menus. My focus was keeping interaction simple in VR, so players could stay in the story instead of learning controls.
