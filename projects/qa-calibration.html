Do our QA evaluators agree with each other?

Agents said their QA score depended on who evaluated them. I tested whether that was true.

10.3 ptsbetween the most and least strict evaluator50%agreement on empathy

This is a practice project. The company is fictional and the data is synthetic, generated to behave like real contact-centre data. The questions, methods and code are the ones I would use on the job.

The question

A quality team of six evaluators scores contacts with the same scorecard. Agents’ bonuses depend on the result.

The question: do evaluators give the same contact the same score, and which scorecard items cause disagreement?

The data

A calibration session: 6 evaluators each scored the same 30 recorded contacts, item by item.

What I did

  1. Used the median of the six scores as the consensus score for each contact.
  2. Measured each evaluator’s average difference from consensus, to find who is stricter or more lenient.
  3. Measured, for each scorecard item, how often all evaluators gave the same pass or fail.

What I found

Two evaluators are out of line

E4 scores 6.3 points above consensus on average and E5 4 points below. Between them, the same contact can differ by 10.3 points, which is enough to change an agent’s bonus.

The scorecard itself is unclear in two places

Evaluators agree on greeting 90% of the time but only 50% on empathy and 53.3% on case notes. Those items need examples of what a pass looks like.

What I would recommend

  • Run monthly calibration sessions and track each evaluator’s difference from consensus.
  • Rewrite empathy and case notes with clear pass and fail examples.
  • Hold bonus decisions for an agent when their scores come mostly from one out-of-line evaluator.
