Does an onboarding tour keep new members longer?

A wellness app added a short guided tour for new members in May. I measured whether it changed how long they stayed.

+16.9 ptsactive members at month 6+7 ptsmonth-3 retention after launch

This is a practice project. The company is fictional and the data is synthetic, generated to behave like real product data. The questions, methods and code are the ones I would use on the job.

The question

Many new members leave in the first month: 24% of those who skip the tour are gone after month one.

The question: do members who see the tour stay longer, and does it show in the cohorts since it launched?

The data

Nine monthly signup cohorts (January to September), each member’s active months, and whether they completed the tour.

What I did

  1. Built a cohort table in SQL: for each signup month, the share of members still active after each month.
  2. Compared cohorts before and after the tour launched in May.
  3. Compared retention curves of members who saw the tour and members who skipped it.
  4. Designed the cohort view in Figma first, so it would read clearly before building it in Tableau.

What I found

Cohorts since May stay longer

Month-3 retention averaged 69.7% for January to April cohorts and 76.7% for cohorts from May on: 7 points more.

The gap grows over time

By month 6, 68.3% of members who saw the tour are still active, against 51.4% who skipped it. Most of the difference is created in the first month.

What I would recommend

  • Make the tour harder to skip, and shorter, so more members finish it.
  • Add a check-in message at the end of month one, when most members leave.
  • Keep a small group without the tour for a few months, to measure the effect cleanly.
